MALEVIL


VIDEO - 2022

A fictional creation for a film festival called Nowhere Film Festival. Brutalist designs and illustrations mixed together to create organized chaos. This project is part of my weekly project sprint, where the goal is to force myself to keep my mind running and to create something, even if it's simple.


Posters, business cards, and merchandising made to fit the atmosphere of this year's edition of Nowhere.
